FAQ

What changes, and what stays?

We change how your site looks and sounds, never how your system runs.

We change: colors, fonts and layout; headlines and page copy, written in your voice; your story, mission and vision; photos, offers and calls to action.

We leave exactly as it is: your funnels and pipelines, workflows and automations, calendars and integrations, contacts and CRM data.

Will you touch my automations?

No. We change how your pages look and read. Your funnels, workflows, calendars and contacts stay exactly as they are.

How do you get into my GHL account? Do I share my password?

No password needed. You add us as a user on your GHL sub-account, and you can remove our access anytime.

What do I need to send?

Your logo, colors, fonts, photos and offers, plus anything that describes your business: a brochure, a price list, your old website. No brand kit yet? Tell us when you request your mockup and we’ll talk through options.

What does “7 days or less” mean?

Seven business days from the day we have your access and materials. Weekends aren’t counted.

What if I don’t love it?

You get up to 2 rounds of revisions, so you can tell us what to change before it goes live.

Is hosting included?

Your site stays in your own GHL account. You keep paying GoHighLevel and your domain as usual; there’s no extra hosting fee from us.

What if I want to make changes weeks or months after you hand it to me?

Add our optional care plan: $20 a month for 2 small edits a month and weekly group office hours. Prefer a one-off session? Consults are $50 for 30 minutes or $100 for an hour. Skip both and your site keeps running.

Do you do landing pages too?

Yes. One page that sells your offer, with the same brand fit and voice, for $299. It includes 1 round of revisions and goes live in 3 business days, inside your own GHL account.

Can my coach or agency buy this for our whole group?

Yes. Partners pay the same $499 per website and can add their own markup.

Is the mockup really free?

Yes. We redesign the top section of your homepage in your brand, with a watermark, so you can see the difference. Love it? We build the rest for $499. Not for you? No charge, no follow-up spam.
